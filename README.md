## What It Does

It takes the word list given for the QRT challenge, and assigns each a FEN board string. You can either encode words into FEN strings, which are then displayed to be re-created on physical boards, or you can upload images of the physical boards to decode to the original word.

## Inspiration

We were inspired by the QRT physical encoding challenge. We wanted to think of every day objects that wouldn't look out of place in lots of places, or in photos, while also having lots of potential for encoding. We remembered that chess boards have famously many game states, so decided to go with this.

## Accomplishments We're Proud Of

- Finding and implementing a library which allowed us to build bitmaps of the chess icons, allowing both humans and the program to recognise the same piece in a clean manner.
- A generally much better project than we managed last year!

## What's next for Chess Encoding

In an ideal world, we could have a magnetic system that moves pieces to the correct positions automatically when entering a word to encode, or you can manually place the pieces which are then detected on the board, and decoded.
